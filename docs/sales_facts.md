# Epicode sales facts

## Who we are
Epicode builds carrier-grade telephony and Voice AI infrastructure:
IraVoice (media server, dialer and telephony stack), BotCompose (voicebot
orchestration) and Campaign Manager (outbound campaigns). Available
white-labelled for partners.

## Scale and performance
- Up to 1,650 concurrent streaming calls on a single 8-core VM with 8 GB
  RAM (about 1,000 concurrent calls with full recording).
- High-availability, active-active deployment across geo-redundant data
  centres, with horizontal scaling for large call volumes.
- TLS / Secure RTP for signalling and media.

## IraVoice
- Hyperscalable, bidirectional media server: PBX, dialer, call recorder,
  live monitoring and reporting in one stack.
- SIP trunk management, load balancing and real-time QoS monitoring.
- Cloud, on-premise or hybrid deployment with dedicated resources.
- Real-time call events, CDRs and recordings pushed to S3, GCP, Azure or
  any HTTP endpoint.

## BotCompose
- Enterprise voicebot orchestration across any STT, TTS and LLM
  provider, or custom models, so customers are never locked into one AI
  vendor.
- Switch AI models at runtime, VAD-based speech optimisation, and TTS
  response caching that cuts latency and AI cost.
- Full analytics: transcripts, latency metrics, outcomes.
- Integrates with IraVoice, enterprise PBX, PSTN, WhatsApp, WebRTC and
  CRM/HRMS systems.

## Campaign Manager
- Bulk contact import and dialling with campaign-level concurrency,
  retry rules, call windows and business-hour controls.
- Day-end MIS and dashboards.

## Why partners work with us
- Add Voice AI and outbound calling to their platform without building
  the telephony layer.
- Vendor-neutral AI layer: bring their own STT/TTS/LLM.
- White-label option, so it ships under their brand.

## Trusted by
Zoom, Five9, Bajaj Finserv, Pinnacle.

## The demo (15-20 minutes)
A live voicebot call, the Campaign Manager dashboard, live monitoring
with recordings and transcripts, and a walkthrough of how it plugs into
their stack.

## Writing style for follow-ups
- Lead with what it means for their business, not our features.
- One idea per email; keep it short and specific to them.
- End with one clear ask (a call, a demo, or a time that suits them).
